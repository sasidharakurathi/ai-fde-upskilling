# Consistency System

You said you procrastinate a lot. This system is built around that. It aims to make **starting
easy** and **restarting easy**. It does not demand willpower.

## When I study
**Flexible: whenever I have free time.** Safety net: if I haven't started by **bedtime**, I do the
15-minute bad-day minimum (below) before sleeping. It still counts, and the streak stays alive.

## The daily loop (≈1 h on weekdays)
1. **Open `CHECKINS.md`** and read yesterday's *"First step tomorrow"* line. It is ≤ 5 minutes of work.
   Do only that first.
2. **Set a 25-minute timer** and work on today's lesson in `lessons/` (or the Saturday project). After the
   timer, decide: continue or stop. Either is fine.
3. **Stop at 75 minutes** even if the lesson isn't finished. A lesson may take 2–3 days, and that's fine.
4. **Commit** your work.
5. **Check in:** tell Claude "X done" (or what you did). Claude writes the entry, including your *First step tomorrow*.

## Day statuses
| Mark | Meaning | Counts for streak? |
|------|---------|--------------------|
| ✅ | Did the planned task (or a real chunk of it) | Yes |
| 🟡 | **Bad-day minimum** (15 min, see below) | Yes |
| 💤 | **Planned** rest day, declared in the previous weekly review (max 1/week) | Doesn't break it, doesn't add to it |
| ❌ | Missed | Resets current streak |

## 🟡 The bad-day rule
Tired, busy, sick, or just not feeling it? **Don't skip. Shrink.** Do **15 minutes** of ONE of these:
- Re-read one lesson's notes in `notes/lesson-notes.md`.
- Re-run one of your old scripts and change one small thing (a feature, a prompt, a threshold).
- Fix or clean **one** small thing in the code (a name, a test, a README line).
- Write a precise *"First step tomorrow"* for the planned task.
- Read today's lesson's **What you'll build** section and its **New words** table.

Log it as 🟡. That's a win, not a failure. On a bad day the goal is only to keep the habit going.

## Never miss twice
Missing one day happens. **Missing two in a row is how habits die.** So after any ❌, the next day's
only requirement is a 🟡. Lower the bar so you come back.

## No catch-up doubling
There are no dates in this plan, so there's nothing to "catch up" on. Never cram two lessons into one day
to make up for lost time. You simply continue from the lesson you were on. Going slowly doesn't matter. Stopping does.

## Extra lesson on a good day (allowed)
Got spare time and energy? You may do **one extra lesson** (max 2 lessons per day), if:
1. You finish the previous lesson's `Correction:` lines first, then take a 10–15 min break.
2. The 75-minute limit still applies to each lesson.
3. **It never replaces the next day.** Tomorrow you still show up (next lesson, or the 15-minute minimum).

## Restart protocol (after 3+ missed days)
1. No guilt, no backlog. Message Claude: **"restart"** (or edit this file's log below yourself).
2. Do a 20-minute review: re-read the last lesson's notes and re-run its code.
3. Resume at the exact lesson where you stopped. If it's been 2+ weeks, re-read the previous lesson first.
4. Next weekly review: write one sentence on what caused the gap and one change to prevent it.

## Weekly review (Sunday, ~30 min)
Claude drafts it in `WEEKLY_REVIEWS.md` from your check-ins. You read it (about 10 min) and, if you like, add one line:
what got in the way, and one change for next week. Declare next week's 💤 day if you want one.

## Metrics that matter
- **Consistency %** = days ✅ or 🟡 ÷ days planned this week. **Target: ≥ 80%.** This matters more than the streak.
- **Current / longest streak**: motivation, not judgement.
- **Lessons completed** and **stages passed**.
